# NurseNova

**Nursing education presentations, organised by subject — [nursenova.duckdns.org](https://nursenova.duckdns.org)**

NurseNova is a free study site for nursing students and educators. Faculty share presentations; students study them
slide by slide, take practice questions, keep private notes and track their progress — on any phone or computer.

- **Browse by subject:** Anatomy and Physiology, Medical-Surgical Nursing, Child Health, Midwifery and Obstetrics,
  Community Health, Mental Health, Nursing Care Plans and more — [explore all presentations](https://nursenova.duckdns.org/explore)
- **Courses:** presentations grouped into study courses — [see courses](https://nursenova.duckdns.org/courses)
- **For GNM and B.Sc Nursing students**, and for exam preparation
- **Membership:** extra members-only content, free during the launch offer — [membership](https://nursenova.duckdns.org/membership)
